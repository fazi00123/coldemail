FULL PLAIN-TEXT RESEARCH
==================================================

Sundial Solar is a locally owned and family-managed renewable energy contractor based in Concord, New Hampshire, operating at 7 Pleasant St. Extension. Founded in 2015, the company has completed hundreds of installations across New Hampshire, Vermont, Maine, and Massachusetts. Sundial Solar is led by President/Owner Ryan Young alongside Kevin Lehman, who serves as VP of Sales and Marketing.

Sundial Solar positions itself as a turnkey electrification partner. Rather than outsourcing physical labor to external subcontractors, Sundial Solar conducts system design, engineering, state/local permitting, utility interconnection, and installation using their own licensed in-house electricians and installers. Their services range from residential and commercial solar panel installation to battery backup solutions, geothermal heating & cooling, and high-efficiency heat pumps.

The company holds an EnergySage Approved Installer status, verifying that they have passed screening for insurance, licensing, and professional standing. Sundial Solar works closely with the National Energy Improvement Fund (NEIF) to provide financing for energy audits and solar installations.
