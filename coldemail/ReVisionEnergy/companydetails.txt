FULL PLAIN-TEXT RESEARCH
==================================================

ReVision Energy is a premier, full-service renewable energy contractor operating in northern New England, with NH branches located in Brentwood and Enfield. Founded in 2003 by Phil Coupe and Fortunat Mueller, the company has established itself as a leading authority in clean energy installation, including solar panels, heat pumps, Tesla Powerwall battery storage, and EV charging stations.

In 2017, ReVision Energy made the transition to becoming 100% employee-owned through an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP). This structure makes all employees equity partners in the firm, fostering a high level of dedication, quality, and service consistency. ReVision is also a Certified B Corporation since 2015, meaning it meets rigorous verified standards of social and environmental performance, public transparency, and legal accountability.

The company operates with 300+ employee-owners. Phil Coupe and Fortunat Mueller are the primary founders. Fortunat Mueller (P.E.) serves as President/CEO, although he is currently on a year-long sabbatical as of late 2024, with Daniel Clapp stepping in to lead the company in the interim. The Brentwood facility at 7 Commercial Drive also houses the ReVision Energy Technical Center (RETC), led by Master Electrician Nathan Poland, which functions as an in-house apprenticeship school to train journeyman electricians.

ReVision Energy has a stellar public reputation, boasting a 4.9-star rating on Google Maps with over 200 customer reviews. They also maintain an A+ rating on the Better Business Bureau.
