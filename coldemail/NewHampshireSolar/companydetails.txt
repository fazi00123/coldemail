FULL PLAIN-TEXT RESEARCH
==================================================

New Hampshire Solar (doing business as NH Solar) is a highly specialized, locally owned solar installation company based in New Hampton, New Hampshire. Founded in 2017 by owner Peter Hall, the company intentionally maintains a small-scale, customer-centric operational structure. Peter Hall's business philosophy focuses on building direct, personalized relationships with clients and keeping the service area within a 30-mile radius of New Hampton to ensure top service quality.

NH Solar specializes in complex solar systems, including high-efficiency bifacial ground-mounted arrays and fully off-grid configurations. They work extensively with leading storage technologies such as SimpliPhi LFP and Homegrid batteries, coupled with Sol-Ark hybrid inverters. They are direct-to-owner operators contactable at peter@nh.solar or through their office phone at 603-968-6031.
