FULL PLAIN-TEXT RESEARCH SUMMARY
==================================================

COMPANY OVERVIEW:
Seventh Gen Solar (legal name: 7th Gen Solar, LLC) is a family-owned and operated solar
energy installation and electrical services company based in Canterbury/Boscawen, New
Hampshire. The business is registered with an address of 1 Fisher Ave, Boscawen, NH 03303
and has been operating for 10+ years per their own website, though legal incorporation via
the LLC structure occurred on November 3, 2020. The company serves the Northern New England
region including New Hampshire, Maine, and Vermont.

OWNERSHIP:
The company is owned and operated by Nathan "Nate" Preisendorfer, who is listed as the
principal contact/member in BBB records and is the owner of the email Nate@SeventhGenSolar.com,
which was publicly identified in a Facebook post. The business is described as family-owned
and operated. No verified professional LinkedIn profile was found for Nathan Preisendorfer.

SERVICES:
Seventh Gen Solar offers full-service solar PV integration for residential and commercial
customers. Services include: net-metered solar systems, off-grid solar systems, hybrid battery
backup systems, roof/ground/pole mount solar arrays, dual-axis tracking units, EV charging
station installations, whole-home standby generator installations, mini-split AC systems, and
general residential and commercial electrical work. The company also performs post-installation
maintenance and repairs, which is specifically praised in customer reviews.

LOCATION AND SERVICE AREA:
Primary operating location is the Canterbury/Boscawen, NH area. Service area covers NH, ME,
and VT. The company is accessible at (603) 731-4777 (primary) and (603) 717-6367 (secondary).
Website: www.seventhgensolar.com

REPUTATION AND REVIEWS:
The company has a positive reputation. Customer reviews on EnergySage and SolarReviews highlight
professionalism, clear communication, quality of work, and strong post-installation support.
One notable review praised the company's responsiveness in solving a major inverter issue after
initial installation. Employee reviews on Indeed describe a supportive, values-driven workplace.
The company is NOT BBB accredited but maintains a BBB profile. They appear on EnergySage as a
verified installer, which is a rigorous marketplace.

COMMUNITY INVOLVEMENT:
Seventh Gen Solar has been noted for charitable work — specifically donating time and equipment
to install solar panels at a Veterans Memorial Recreation Area lodge in New Hampshire, demonstrating
community engagement and a values-based approach to business.

SOCIAL PRESENCE:
The company maintains an active Facebook page under "Seventh Gen Solar, LLC." They post updates
about solar installations, job openings, and community projects. They also reference Instagram
(#7thGenSolar) but a confirmed direct Instagram URL was not found during this research. No LinkedIn
company page or personal page for the owner was found. The company is listed on EnergySage and
SolarReviews as professional platforms.

LICENSING:
The company operates as an electrical contractor in New Hampshire and has been issued electrical
permits (confirmed in Town of Henniker public documents). NH electrical contractor licensing can
be verified at the NH OPLC portal: https://online-licensing.nh.gov/

WEBSITE OBSERVATIONS:
The company website (www.seventhgensolar.com) appears to be built on the DudaOne / DM Direct
platform. It presents as a professional multi-page site with service pages, a contact form,
and branding that uses deep teal/green tones (CSS color: rgba(17,61,60,1)). The site uses
Raleway and Poppins fonts with uppercase headings, professional CTA buttons, and mobile-responsive
design. The site uses a contact form rather than displaying an email address directly — making
the discovered email Nate@SeventhGenSolar.com particularly valuable as it was only found via
Facebook.
