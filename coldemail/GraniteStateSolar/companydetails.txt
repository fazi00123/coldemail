FULL PLAIN-TEXT RESEARCH
==================================================

Granite State Solar, founded in 2008 by Alan Gauntt, is an award-winning residential and commercial solar energy provider headquartered in Bow, New Hampshire. The company has served the New Hampshire and Vermont regions for nearly two decades, transitioning from early solar thermal projects to custom-designed photovoltaic (PV) solar installations. 

Led by President Paul Lesure and General Manager Ben Soucy, Granite State Solar is highly regarded for its 100% in-house engineering and installation team (using certified master electricians rather than subcontractors) and its "no-pressure" educational sales model. The company specializes in rooftop solar, ground mounts, Tesla Powerwall battery backups, EV chargers, and standby generator integration. 

They are an A+ Accredited Business with the BBB and hold Enphase Platinum Installer status. Customer inquiries and site evaluation bookings are directed through their central verified inbox at info@granitestatesolar.com. They maintain active client engagement channels on Facebook, LinkedIn, and YouTube.
