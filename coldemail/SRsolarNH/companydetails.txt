FULL PLAIN-TEXT RESEARCH
==================================================

SRsolarNH (operating as SR Solar) is a premier, family-owned solar energy installation company based in Manchester and Candia, New Hampshire. Founded and co-owned by Josh and Adrianne Koehler, the company has established a reputation for top-tier custom designs, completing over 400 successful residential and commercial installations across New Hampshire, Maine, and Massachusetts.

Rather than offering rigid pre-designed packages, SRsolarNH tailors each solar array (rooftop or ground-mounted) to the client's specific energy usage, roof layout, and budget. In addition to premium solar panels, they specialize in battery storage backups (authorized installers for Tesla Powerwall, FranklinWH, and Schneider Home Boost), EV charging stations, off-grid systems, and standby generator integration. 

The company's dedication to craftsmanship and transparent customer service earned them EnergySage's prestigous Installer of the Year award for 2025 and 2026. The founders remain directly involved in day-to-day operations: Josh Koehler manages project design and sales (contactable at Josh@SRsolarNH.com or 603-540-4243), while Adrianne Koehler directs the administrative office (contactable at Adrianne@SRsolarNH.com or 603-425-9323). They maintain an active Facebook page showcasing their installations, job postings, and community interactions.
