603 Solar LLC is a premier, co-founder-operated residential and commercial solar PV design and installation company headquartered at 24 Charter St, Exeter, NH. Founded in 2018 by Zachary Haithcock, Sean Carlson, and Scot Johnson, the company has grown to become the #1 Solar Company in the "Best of the 603" awards. They are highly active across NH, Southern Maine, and Northern MA. Zachary Haithcock leads operations and is contactable directly at zach@603solar.com (603-582-6032), while co-owner Sean Carlson is contactable at sean@603solar.com (603-978-2228), and operations manager Erin Pierce is at erin@603solar.com.
